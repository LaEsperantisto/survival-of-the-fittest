# CH raised to 160 globally; playable sky is per-planet (worldH)

Silvaris needs very tall mountains (~150) and deep oceans (floors near y 20), which do not fit the old 96-block world height. Decided: the chunk/light array capacity `CH` rises to **160 for every planet**, while each planet carries a `worldH` (Desertis 96, Silvaris 160) used for terrain ceilings, flight ceiling and sky — terrain never generates above its planet's `worldH`. Rejected: a per-world `CH` threading through the chunk meshing, light baking and delta indexing (50+ call sites of array arithmetic, high regression risk for zero Desertis benefit), and cramming the new planet into 96 (the oceans and peaks were the point). Consequence: Desertis pays ~65% more memory per chunk and a little light/mesh work above its terrain line; that cost was weighed and accepted.
