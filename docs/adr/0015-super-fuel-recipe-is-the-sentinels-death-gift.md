# Super Rocket Fuel's recipe is the Sentinel's death-gift — knowledge-gated, not loot-gated

Phase 2 closes when the player refines their own Super Rocket Fuel, but the recipe would otherwise sit open at the Fuel Works long before the story's finale (it already does: `crafting.ts` exposes `super_fuel` from the first visit to Silvaris), making the boss optional flavor. Decided: the **kill teaches the recipe** — the Catalog hides Super Rocket Fuel on BOTH planets until the Sentinel dies, and its dying broadcast (the founders' fuel works) is the unlock. Super Fuel never loots and never trades; the Phase-1 quest canister stays the only canister that exists before the finale. The Nav Core, by contrast, is learnable BEFORE the kill (from the Keepers' final chapter): the player knows why they hunt the Sentinel and prepares with everything except the fuel itself.

Rejected: kill-loot gating (Sentinel drops unique components that ARE the recipe inputs — works, but makes the finale an inventory transaction instead of a story beat, and post-kill crafting would still need the open recipe). The unlock is knowledge: after the kill, both planets' fuel plants can be built and taught.
