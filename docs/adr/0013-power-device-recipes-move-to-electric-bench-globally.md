# Power/device recipes move to the Electric Bench globally — no Crafting Bench fallback

Every new planet gets advanced crafting stations, and the power/device family (Panels, Poles, Batteries, generators, machines, Chests, Regulators, Storm Breakers) deserves a real home instead of cluttering the general Crafting Bench. Decided: those recipes' **one home becomes the new Electric Bench, globally** — the Crafting Bench stops making them even on Desertis. This is deliberate global consistency: one recipe, one station, every planet, so the Catalog never shows two doors to the same item. Nothing Phase-1-critical is stranded — the Crafting Bench keeps making itself, tools, Frames, the Yoke, Refinery, Campfire, Cooking Pot and the Thruster Nozzle — and the Electric Bench is itself a Crafting Bench recipe, cheap and early. Rejected: a Crafting Bench fallback duplicate (breaks the one-home-per-recipe rule and double-lists every power item); gating the Electric Bench to Silvaris only (would brick an old Desertis save wanting a fresh Battery). Consequence: an updated old save wanting a new power part must first build an Electric Bench — guided by the Catalog and Codex, which already cross-link every item's station home.
