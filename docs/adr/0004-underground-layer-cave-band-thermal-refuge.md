# The underground layer: fixed cave band, thermal refuge, shared structure lattice

The desert got a below-ground layer: 3D-noise Caves occupying a fixed y≈8–24 band under every column, with surface mouths only where the biome is rocky, Canyon or Badlands; Ore Veins (iron, copper, Alabaster) crusting cave stone; rare Cave Pools; and procedurally room-generated Dungeons (sealed findable entrance + rooms + enemies) stamped once per Town-lattice cell. Deep Air Temperature is deliberately near-constant (≈ mild): caves trade darkness and enemies for thermal refuge, which is the reason the Heat game pushes you down there. Alternatives considered — caves scattered by local noise at any depth (rejected: the ore-depth contract, especially Cobalt at y ≤ 14, needs a known band), and Dungeons on their own rarer lattice (rejected: reusing the Town lattice keeps discovery pacing to one known clock, TOWN_L=320).
