# Survival of the Fittest

A browser voxel-survival game. You wake up in a crashed ship in the middle of a desert. The wreck is a small, rounded hull with broken wings: a forward cockpit with an insulating-glass windscreen, a central hall with the wreck's crafting bench, a save room whose stern door is the wreck's only opening to the outside, a water compartment whose tank is a pool sunk into the ground, and a regulator room with a pre-installed Heat Regulator. The hull and doors keep sun and wind out, but a sealed space still drifts to the desert's temperature — the desert swings from ~52 °C at midday peak down to ~2 °C at night — unless Heat Regulators hold it neutral. Only the small regulator room starts conditioned. No hunger. Water, heat, health — keep all three in their zones.

## Run it

```sh
npm install
npm run dev        # dev server
npm run build      # typecheck + production bundle in dist/
npm run preview    # serve the built game
```

The build is fully static — `dist/` can be dropped on GitHub Pages or any static host.

## How it works

- **Bars.** Water drains steadily (faster when hot, sprinting, or overencumbered). Low water dims and vignettes your vision; empty water does damage. Body temperature drifts toward the environment: stand under open sky at noon and you cook, stand in the cold at night and you freeze — and inside a sealed space it moves on you three times faster. Body out of the 36–37.5 °C safe zone stops regen; past ~39.5 °C or below ~31.5 °C you take damage. Health at 0 = death. Best days-survived is stored locally.
- **Shelter is physical.** Partial cover is the sum of block thermal resistance straight above you (see `docs/adr/0002-sky-column-shelter-model.md`) — lean-tos, overhangs and insulating glass help. But a fully sealed space equalizes with the outside air: walls alone never save you. A craftable Heat Regulator (one is already installed in the wreck's small regulator room) holds a sealed room at body-neutral temperature — 100 open cells per Regulator, capacity stacks. Sealed means sealed: an open door disables the Regulators and the desert gets in. Aim at an active Regulator and press E to draw on it: your body snaps back to 37 °C on the spot, no drinking required — an idle Regulator does nothing.
- **World.** Infinite chunked voxel desert (1 voxel = 1 m), seeded and deterministic: big rolling dunes, rocky hills and tall buttes, salt flats, stepped mesas cut by deep canyons, round Oasis pools with a bowl-rounded bottom and a living ring of Grass over Dirt — water you can fill canisters from (it is a real liquid: Sources spread and fall, and breaking a Source drains its Flow for good) — oasis pools carry ripe Dune Melon vines, fruiting cacti and Living Trees, and dead-wood groves stand elsewhere. Empty adobe Towns on a rough 320 m lattice: houses with beds, overgrown Farm Land on real soil, shade trees, and Supply Chests holding the dwellers' seeds — Tree Seeds included — and stores (each cluster's stores are there exactly once). Cacti heavy with prickly pears (and they actually sting when you press into them), and wildlife — desert snakes that hunt anything that steps too close, kangaroo rats that bolt when you see them, Sand Vultures that only dare prey that is already wounded, slow Thornback Tortoises, quick Fennec Foxes, and the deep-Cave Scorpions and Sand Wurms. Arched Cave Mouths gape in rocky slopes and tunnel straight down into the Caves. A second wreck — and its Engine Core — waits somewhere around 400 m out. On a rough 380 m lattice sit Trader Camps — a windmill tower, a waystation hut, or a farmstead with a water trough — each held by a single robed Trader. A 24-minute day/night cycle drives temperature, sky, sun, moon, and light.
- **Barter.** No coin out here: a Trader swaps goods for goods — your skins, shells, hides, meat and scrap for metal, glass, empty and full canisters, coolant cells and catalysts. Each camp's table carries five offers, fixed for that camp for the whole in-game day and restocked every dawn, so a visit tomorrow morning finds a different five. Stand in front of the stall and press E.
- **Sandstorms.** Without warning the wind rises and the sky goes rust: visibility closes to a few dozen meters, the sun drowns, the noise roars. In the open a storm amplifies whatever the air already is — a scorching noon turns murderous, a freezing night bites harder — sweat doubles and bare skin is scoured. Ship walls, rock shade and the Caves ride it out untouched; out in it, the storm is what kills the expedition, not the day count.
- **Farming.** Press Q with a shovel aimed at Dirt or Grass to till it into Farm Land — sand never holds a furrow — then plant seeds in it. A crop ripens only while water sits within 4 blocks and the air in its cell stays in the crop's temperature band: Desert Wheat tolerates 2–42 °C — night, shade, or a tempered room; Dune Melons want 22–45 °C, i.e. the hot open desert. Melons re-ripen on the vine, wheat cuts back to Farm Land, and ripe cacti (anywhere) can be picked by hand for two Prickly Pears. Planting a Cactus pad also accepts plain sand. Seeds come from oasis loot, Town chests and your own harvests.
- **Wood farms.** A Tree Seed (Town chests, and 20 % of broken Leaves) planted in Dirt or Farm Land grows a Living Tree stage by stage over ~8 minutes as long as water stays within 6 blocks: trunk, branches, canopy. Chopping the trunk yields Wood Log (renewable campfire fuel — a Campfire's "add fuel" takes 3 charges from a Log or ¼ from leaf-scrap Kindling); the seed itself comes back if you pull a sapling. Cacti also grow: a replanted pad climbs over time and matures into fruit-bearing columns.
- **Water is a substance.** Aim at a still Source with a Bucket (craft: 2 metal at the bench) in hand and place/click to scoop it up — flowing water can't be scooped — then pour the Bucket back out anywhere as a new Source. Oasis pools, your farm's irrigation, and buried buckets of water are all movable.
- **Sleep.** A Bed (craft: dead wood + snake skins; spans 2 blocks, one long sleep per game day) takes eight hours in the dark to the cool of the morning — a "NAP READY" chip lights when the bed is ready again, and the clock skips to morning.
- **Loop.** Follow the quest chain (step under open sky → strip the wreck → find an oasis → carry a canister to a beacon → craft a tool → grow and harvest one crop → reach a Town and open its Supply Chest → find the second wreck (the Catalyst drops there) → break free its Engine Core → set it within 10 m of a Save Beacon) tracked in the top-right HUD; block-shaped rewards land as crash pods from the sky. Explore, salvage debris and full water canisters around the wreck, dig up buried canisters, manage a carry-weight budget, campfires (warmth + light, fuel with wood), craft at the wreck's bench — or build your own two-block Crafting Bench anywhere. Eat to stay alive: break cacti for prickly pears (+8 Water each), harvest melons (+30 Water, +5 Health), cook wheat and meat at a campfire (+25 Health cooked), stitch snake skins into Reptile Armour (40% of all damage shrugs off). Fight bare-handed or with any tool, or forge the Metal Sword (bench: 2 Iron Rods + 1 Dead Wood) — far harder hits and longer reach: two swings kill a snake, one kills a rat. Beacons save your run; death stashes everything you carried and everything you wore in a Death Cache at the spot — respawn at a beacon and go retrieve it. Placing the Engine Core completes the Main Quest: the relay calls rescue and you keep surviving.

## Controls

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| WASD / stick | Move |
| Mouse / drag | Look |
| Shift / Sprint button | Sprint (drinks faster) — on touch, the Sprint button toggles it |
| Space | Jump |
| E | Interact (doors, crops, beds, chests, canisters, benches, campfires, beacons, caches, Heat Regulators, trade at a camp stall) |
| Q | Use the held item: full canister drinks (+25 Water, −1.5 °C body), food eats; a shovel also tills Dirt into Farm Land; a Bucket at a Source scoops it |
| X | Sweat Surge — force your body cool: −10 Water, −1.5 °C body, 10 s cooldown (needs ≥20 Water) |
| Left click (hold) | Mine / break (tool tier matters) — aimed at a mob, attack it |
| Right click / place | Place selected block |
| 1–9 / scroll / click hotbar | Select item — pressing/clicking the already-held one empties your hands |
| I | Inventory |
| P | Pause / resume |
| Esc | Close open menu, skip the guided tour, or pause (save / export world / quit) |

Opening a brand-new world starts a guided in-world tour: a short flyover of the wreck, then hands-on steps through the hatch, the heat, water, the Supply Chest, crafting, mining, farming and Save Beacons (Esc skips it). The full instructions live under Pause → Instructions (or the title screen). On touch, tapping empty inventory space or panel chrome deselects the held item.

Crafting and smelting open through E at a Crafting Bench or Campfire. The active quest shows a waypoint in the HUD — a compass arrow, distance, and a marker beam at the target; a craftable Surveyor's Visor equipped in the Head slot beams every Death Cache, Reward Cache and Supply Chest through walls too. The Catalog (pause menu or title screen) lists every item, block, and recipe.

Inventory: drag items between cells; click a cell to select it (Rotate / Drop buttons appear beside the grid). Shift-click an item to quick-move it into the open chest (either direction); with no chest open it lifts straight into **Temporary Storage**. A drag released anywhere off the grid parks the item in **Temporary Storage** next to it — the inventory can't be closed while it's occupied; drag the item back from there or use its Drop button. Dropped items pile up in front of you (never inside walls) and need a short beat before they can be picked back up. Three Armour Slots sit above the grid — Backpack, Head, Body: select an item and click Equip — or click the empty slot directly — to wear it; click a filled slot to take it off. Backpack I (6 dead wood + 2 metal at a bench) grows the grid from 3×4 to 4×5 and adds +20 kg; a visor's upgrades only work while equipped in the Head slot; Reptile Armour (3 snake skins) in the Body slot shrugs off 40% of all damage. Edible items get an Eat button when selected — prickly pears restore Water, cooked rat restores Health.

Supply Chests are container blocks (craft: 6 dead wood + 1 metal at the bench; the cockpit starts with one). Aim at one and press E: its grid opens above yours, and you can drag items between the two or use **Move to pack**. Chests placed face-to-face merge into one bigger grid — 3×3 per chest, up to three wide, then a row taller per chest (with three chests it's 9×3, four is 9×4…) — and **Chest Connector** rails (craft: 2 dead wood + 1 metal) link chests that can't touch without counting toward the grid themselves. Break one and the grid shrinks; contents that no longer fit spill onto the sand.

Inside the wreck (and anywhere else you build), **Inside Doors** divide rooms for movement and sight — in temperature terms they are just air, and never help seal a space. **Metal Plating** makes interior walls. A sealed room held neutral by Regulators shows a **⚙ REGULATED** chip under the clock.

Touch: on-screen stick + sprint / jump / break / place / act / drink / surge / item / bag buttons and a pause button (top-left). Sprint is a toggle — press it once to run.
One finger steers the camera — extra fingers are ignored. Buttons fire when pressed; Jump and Break act while held — hold BREAK aimed at a mob to fight it (a Metal Sword reaches farther and hits harder).
In the inventory: tap an item to select it, then tap another cell to move it there (the occupant swaps into its place); tap an Armour Slot to wear the selected gear. There is one selection across pack and chest grids at a time — tap a cell in the other grid to pull the selected item across. Tap the Temporary Storage shelf to lift the selected item onto it or send the parked item back to the grid it came from.

## Tech

TypeScript + Vite + Three.js with a hand-rolled chunked voxel renderer (greedy-ish per-chunk meshing, dirty-chunk rebuilds, fog-tuned render distance). Lighting runs in two modes, switchable in Settings: the default bakes a per-chunk skylight flood (sky dimmed by any cover, doubled cost through Leaves; day→moon ambient and lamp point lights in a custom shader, zero scene lights on the world), or real-time directional shadow maps (see `docs/adr/0003-baked-skylight-with-optional-shadow-maps.md`). The sky carries a sun and moon disc on the same arc, with cool moonlight and stars at night. Audio is fully procedural WebAudio — wind bed, and a generative score that rotates through four genres (drone, arpeggio "wanderer", long-line "nightwind", hand-drum "caravan"), each with an instrument voice (saw pad, plucked strings, breathy panpipes, bowed fiddle), a fresh random key and mode every 90–240 s, day/night changing the pools (Music toggle in Settings); event SFX; zero asset files. No workers, no SharedArrayBuffer, no server. World state is delta-saved per slot in localStorage, with JSON export/import for sharing worlds and transferring saves between machines. Design history lives in `docs/adr/`; vocabulary in `GLOSSARY.md`.
